4. E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing e-commerce sales data (2021-2025)
to understand product performance, customer value, regional demand, and trends
Tools
Power BI, Power Query (data cleaning, date transformations), DAX (measures)
 Business Questions Answered
**Products**
1. Which products sell the most?
2. Which products generate the highest revenue?
3. Which products generate the highest profit?
4. Which categories dominate sales?
5. Which brands contribute the most revenue?
6. Which products have strong demand but weak margins?
7. Which categories combine sales growth with profitability?

**Customers & Regions**
8. Which region generates the highest revenue?
9. Who are the highest-value customers and where are they located?
10. Does product demand differ between regions?
11. Which customer group generates the most revenue?
12. Which month generates the highest sales?

## Key DAX Measures
- Total Quantity Sold = SUM(order_items[quantity])
- Total Revenue = SUM(order_items[net_sales])
- Total Profit = SUM(order_items[profit])
- Profit Margin % = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue])
- Sales Growth % = YoY growth using SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR

## Key Insights
- Total revenue: 192.40M
- South region leads with 60.34M
- Jewelry and Home Appliances are the top revenue categories
- Samsung is the top brand by revenue
- Consumer segment generates the most revenue
- USA has the highest quantity sold
